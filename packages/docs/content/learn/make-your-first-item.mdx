In this lesson you make two items, **Berry Dough** and a **Berry Bun**, with recipes that
connect them: craft the dough from wheat and sweet berries, cook it in a furnace, eat the bun.
On the way you paint a texture (with an animation), write recipes without typing JSON, and
see the Java and data files Blocksy writes.

<TextureDemo caption="The bun you're about to make. It has two frames: the second one steams. Try painting on it." />

## Before you start

You need Blocksy with Java set up. If you made the [first block](/docs/learn/make-your-first-block),
you're ready.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the project

On **Home**, click **New project**, pick **Starter item** on the left, name your mod and click
**Create**. The first build downloads Minecraft once; later builds take seconds.

The starter gives you a creative tab, the two items and three recipes, so you can play right
away and then change everything.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paint the texture

Click **Berry Bun** in the project tree. The canvas is now the **texture editor**:

- **Pencil** (B), **Eraser** (E), **Fill** (G) and **Picker** (I) on the left. Right-click
  picks a colour with any tool.
- **Mirror** (M) paints both halves at once: great for symmetrical items.
- The **palette** on the right, plus the colours already in your texture.
- **Frames** at the bottom: add a frame to animate the item. **Onion skin** (O) shows the
  previous frame faintly, so the next one lines up.

Every stroke is one undo step (Ctrl+Z).

<Callout title="Animated textures">
Minecraft animates a texture when its frames are stacked into one tall PNG with a small
`.png.mcmeta` file next to it saying how long each frame shows (in ticks, 20 per second).
Blocksy writes both for you.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Make it food

In the inspector, under **Food**, **Can be eaten** is on. **Nutrition** fills the hunger bar
(2 per drumstick) and **Saturation** decides how long you stay full. Under **Effects**, the bun
gives **Speed** for 10 seconds.

The Java appears under each field, like `.nutrition(6)` and `MobEffects.SPEED`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Read the recipes

Click **Berry Dough** under **Recipes**. The crafting grid shows wheat and sweet berries, and
**Shapeless** means any arrangement works. Click a slot to pick another item or a tag; drag
a slot to move it.

**Berry Bun (furnace)** is a **Smelting** recipe: dough in, bun out, 10 seconds. There's a
**Smoker** version too, which cooks twice as fast.

<VideoLesson id="tpQ3VGUxhp8" title="Fabric Modding Tutorial - Minecraft 1.21: Custom Recipes & Loot Tables" creator="Modding by Kaupenjoe">
  writing recipe JSON by hand, which Blocksy does for you here
</VideoLesson>

</Step>
<Step>

### Save a version

Press **Ctrl+S** (or click **History** under Overview), type *Starter item* and click
**Save version**. Now you can experiment freely: **Restore** brings this moment back.

</Step>
<Step>

### Craft it, cook it, eat it

Click **Run**. When the status bar says **Your mod is loaded**:

1. Make a world in **Survival** (or Creative and `/gamemode survival` later).
2. `/give @s minecraft:wheat` and `/give @s minecraft:sweet_berries`, then craft **Berry Dough**.
3. Put the dough in a **furnace** with some fuel and wait for the **Berry Bun**.
4. Get a little hungry (sprint around), then eat the bun: you're faster for 10 seconds.

Hover the bun to see its tooltip; look closely to see it steam.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What you learned

- An item has an **id** that never changes and a **name** players see. Its texture can be animated.
- **Food** is a property of an item: nutrition, saturation and effects.
- **Recipes** are JSON files in your mod's data. Shaped recipes keep their pattern; shapeless don't.
- **Versions** are snapshots of your project you can go back to.
