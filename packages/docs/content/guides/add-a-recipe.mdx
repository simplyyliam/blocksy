---
title: Add a recipe
description: Make an item craftable, smeltable or cuttable with the visual recipe editor.
---

<RecipeDemo />

1. In the project tree, click **+** next to **Recipes** and give it a name (only you see it).
2. Pick the kind at the top: **Shaped**, **Shapeless**, **Smelting**, **Blasting**, **Smoking** or **Stonecutting**.
3. Click a slot and pick an ingredient. Search finds your mod's items and blocks, Minecraft's
   items, and tags (like `#minecraft:planks`, which accepts every plank).
4. Click the result slot and pick what the recipe makes. Set how many with the number under it.

Until a recipe has a result and at least one ingredient, Blocksy doesn't write it, and says
what's missing under the grid.

<Callout title="Shaped patterns are trimmed">
Empty rows and columns around a shaped pattern are removed, so a 2x2 pattern also works in
the small crafting grid of your inventory.
</Callout>

Cooking recipes have a **time** and the **experience** you get per item in the inspector.
Switch to **Code** to read the JSON: it's the same format as Minecraft's own recipes.
