---
title: Translate your mod
description: Use the lang editor to give your mod's text in other languages.
---

<LanguageDemo />

1. In the project tree, click **+** next to **Languages** and name it, for example *German*.
2. In the inspector, pick the **Language** (or type a code like `de_de`).
3. Fill in the table. Every name, tab title and tooltip line your mod makes is listed with its
   English text. Empty cells stay English in game.

A Language for **English (US)** works a little differently: its text *replaces* the English
Blocksy writes from your element names, key by key. Use **Custom key** for text your own code
will show later.

Players see your translation when they pick that language in Minecraft's options.
